# WHO raw data (not included)

Download from the WHO Mortality Database, section "Download raw data files":
https://www.who.int/data/data-collection-tools/who-mortality-database

Unzip into this folder:

- `Morticd10_part1` ... `Morticd10_part6` (deaths by ICD-10 code, all countries)
- `pop` (population and live births)
- optionally `documentation.zip` (file layout and age formats)

The paper used the files downloaded on 15 November 2026 (file timestamps February 2026).
WHO revises the database, so a later download may give slightly different numbers.

Countries used: Japan (3160), United Kingdom (4308), Germany (4085).
